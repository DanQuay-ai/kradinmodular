Radicals

The base signs: the vowels and the p, t, d, k, f, s, n and r rows. Every other row is built from these with modifiers and glides. Each radical is listed with its numbered strokes, in writing order, and the elements each stroke is made of. Click one to edit it in the stroke generator.
