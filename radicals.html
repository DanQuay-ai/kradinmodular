Radicals

The base signs: the vowels and the p, t, d, k, f, s, n and r rows, drawn in the Construction lab. Every other row is composed from them: a modifier bar grown out of the rightmost element, a bracket, a glide, or the ni prefix. Strokes are numbered in writing order.
