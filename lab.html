Composed glyphs show their radical locked (striped): it follows the approved glyph. Place it, add modifier strokes, or continue one of its strokes with + Extension. Unlock a glyph to edit all its strokes for that glyph only. Delete placeholders you don't need; they leave the font. When every glyph is approved, Export font sends it to the type tester.
